08 — Questions & Answers

Common Questions. Clear Answers.

Are these reviews legal and safe for my account? +
Yes, 100%. We never use software bots or fake profiles. Every review is placed by a real person with an active Amazon or Google account across India, keeping your seller account completely safe.
Why do you use empty boxes? +
Sending empty boxes allows real delivery tracking to verify the purchase on Amazon without you having to sacrifice expensive product inventory. It saves you huge costs.
How do the Google Tap Cards stop bad reviews? +
When a customer taps your card, happy customers (4 or 5 stars) are sent directly to Google. If someone is unhappy (1, 2, or 3 stars), the card sends their feedback to you privately so you can fix it before it ever hurts your public rating.
How quickly will I see sales go up? +
Most clients see their product rank climb higher within 2 to 3 weeks. As seen in our Smartivity and AGARO cases, consistent 5-star reviews regularly double or triple monthly orders.
